# Game Name Brainstorm

**Frontiers of Sol** is the selected game title. Trademark, storefront, domain, and search-confusion clearance remains required before public commercial use. The alternatives below are retained as naming history and possible subtitle/expansion inspiration.

## Naming goals

The name should suggest hands-on spacecraft, entrepreneurial growth, and expansion throughout Sol without promising interstellar travel or making the game sound purely military.

## Considered shortlist

| Candidate | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Frontiers of Sol** | Clear Solar System scope; supports exploration, industry, and political expansion | Slightly strategy-forward; “frontiers” is common in genre titles |
| **The Long Ascent** | Human, memorable, fits company-to-civilization progression | Less explicitly space/economy focused |
| **Ascendant Orbit** | Connects flight with rising power | Slightly abstract |
| **Solward** | Short, active, and suggests outward movement through the system | May imply a destination at the Sun; likely needs a subtitle |
| **Engines of Ascent** | Strong hands-on engineering and company-growth metaphor | Does not immediately communicate economy or the Solar System |
| **Orbital Ambition** | Captures private-company beginnings and growth | “Orbital” undersells later interplanetary reach |
| **Reach of Sol** | Compact and broad enough for exploration through empire | Can sound territorial or combat-focused |

## More frontier and exploration directions

| Candidate | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Horizons of Sol** | Optimistic, broad, and less conquest-oriented | Softer engineering identity |
| **The Solar Frontier** | Immediately readable | Very generic and potentially crowded |
| **Sol: Open Frontier** | Signals sandbox freedom and setting | Subtitle-like construction and generic wording |
| **Outward Through Sol** | Strong expansion arc | Longer and less title-like |
| **The Human Frontier** | Emphasizes civilization and people | Does not explicitly identify space or Sol |
| **Beyond Earthbound** | Clear departure-from-Earth theme | Awkward phrasing and weaker late-game identity |
| **The Expanding Horizon** | Fits technological and corporate growth | Does not clearly signal spaceflight |

## More engineering and flight directions

| Candidate | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Vector of Ascent** | Technical, active, and flight-forward | Abstract and less economic |
| **Ascent Vector** | Shorter engineering tone | Sounds like a subsystem or company |
| **Flightpath: Sol** | Communicates piloting and Solar System scope | Stylized punctuation; undersells construction |
| **Launch Window** | Authentic spaceflight term and memorable | Focuses on one mission-planning concept |
| **Escape Vector** | Energetic engineering phrase | Can imply leaving Sol, contrary to intended scope |
| **Built for Orbit** | Directly promises construction and flight | Undersells the later Solar System game |
| **Machines of Sol** | Connects spacecraft and industry to the setting | Less human and exploration-oriented |

## More company and civilization directions

| Candidate | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Helios Venture** | Strong private-company and space tone | “Helios” is commonly used |
| **Solar Endeavor** | Broad exploration and enterprise tone | Generic wording |
| **Aphelion Industries** | Distinct industrial/outer-system flavor | Sounds like a company/factory game more than flight |
| **Orbital Enterprise** | Clearly communicates business plus spaceflight | Descriptive and potentially generic |
| **Venture Beyond** | Optimistic company-growth theme | Does not identify the Solar System |
| **Age of Sol** | Fits the civilization-scale late game | Understates direct construction and flight |
| **Ascendancy of Sol** | Strong rise-to-power arc | Sounds heavily grand-strategic or militaristic |
| **Beyond the Cradle** | Strong humanity-expansion theme | Common phrase and less spacecraft-specific |

## Possible title/subtitle pairs

- **Frontiers of Sol: The Long Ascent**
- **Frontiers of Sol: Orbital Ambition**
- **Solward: A Spaceflight Company Simulator**
- **Engines of Ascent: Frontiers of Sol**
- **The Long Ascent: A Frontier of Sol**

The subtitle can carry the company/engineering promise if the primary title emphasizes Solar System scale. It should not be locked before the core tone and marketing audience are clearer.

`SolProject` remains the repository codename; **Frontiers of Sol** is the game title.

## Decisions

| Decision | Status | Why |

| Select Frontiers of Sol as the game title | Confirmed | User selection on 2026-08-12 |
