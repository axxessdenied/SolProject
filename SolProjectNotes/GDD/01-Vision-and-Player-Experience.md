# 01 — Frontiers of Sol: Vision and Player Experience

## Player fantasy

The player is the founder and operational leader of a small private spaceflight company. At first, success is intimate: a vehicle they personally designed survives ignition, responds in flight, reaches an intended trajectory, and returns useful data. Over time, those hard-won capabilities become a company, infrastructure network, industrial base, and political force spread across the Solar System.

## Target emotional arc

- **Curiosity:** “What is there, and what could I learn?”
- **Ingenuity:** “Can I build a craft that accomplishes this with what I have?”
- **Tension:** “Will the design and my flying survive the environment?”
- **Mastery:** “I understand why this worked and can improve it.”
- **Ownership:** “This network exists because of the machines, people, and choices I assembled.”
- **Perspective:** “My company has become one of the forces shaping human civilization.”

## Priority rule

If a later strategic feature reduces the need to design and understand spacecraft, it must justify that cost. Automation should remove repetition after the player demonstrates mastery, while new missions, environments, and technologies continue to create design problems.

## Tone

The proposed tone is optimistic but grounded. Expansion offers wonder and prosperity, while distance, environment, logistics, human needs, commercial pressure, and politics create consequences. The game need not be a comedy or a bleak survival story; the final tone remains open.

## What the game is not

- Not a multiplayer space MMO.
- Not primarily a colony-management game.
- Not primarily a fleet-combat or conquest game.
- Not an interstellar empire game in the planned scope.
- Not a pure cockpit simulator with fixed vehicles.
- Not a spreadsheet grand-strategy game that abstracts away craft design and flight.
- Not a universal engine technology demo.

## Decisions

| Decision | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Founder-led private space company fantasy | Confirmed | User-defined start and growth path |
| Construction and flight remain relevant after strategy unlocks | Proposed | Protects the two highest priorities |
| Optimistic but grounded tone | Proposed | Fits exploration, engineering, and political consequence |
| Exact visual/narrative tone | Open | Needs user direction |
