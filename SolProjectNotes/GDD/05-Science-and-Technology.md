# 05 — Science and Technology

## Design goal

Research should depend on learning something about the Solar System and on solving engineering problems, not simply waiting for a timer or repeating identical clicks.

## Science loop

1. Identify a question, phenomenon, resource prospect, or contract need.
2. Select suitable instruments, location, conditions, duration, and return/transmission method.
3. Build and fly a capable mission.
4. Gather data with quality determined by conditions and instrument operation.
5. Transmit preliminary results or recover higher-value samples/data where applicable.
6. Convert evidence into understanding that satisfies research prerequisites and improves future planning.

Repeated measurements should have a reason—coverage, precision, time variation, calibration, confirmation, or commercial surveying—rather than infinite identical rewards.

## First contract: Orbital Environmental Survey

The first-playable contract gives the initial company a concrete reason to reach orbit without requiring recovery:

1. Build an uncrewed chemical launch vehicle carrying a radiation monitor, magnetometer, upper-atmosphere sensor, power, and communications.
2. Reach an approximately 200 km by 200 km orbit; the final numerical acceptance band is a balance parameter to be set in the first-playable milestone.
3. Remain in a stable orbit for one complete revolution so each instrument can record a valid observation window.
4. Transmit the radiation, magnetic-field, and upper-atmosphere data to the contracted tracking service.
5. Receive funds and research progress that unlock a meaningfully different design option.

Reentry, payload recovery, and crew survival are not requirements for this contract. Instrument conditions and transmission validity must be explained before launch and during flight rather than evaluated through hidden rules.

## Research structure

The proposed research model is a dependency network with branches and prerequisites, not a single linear ladder. Unlocks may require a combination of scientific evidence, engineering capability, facilities, money, partnerships/licenses, and prior technologies.

Technological eras range from a modern chemical-rocket start through fusion and antimatter, with more speculative technology later. Unlocks should introduce new design decisions and operational possibilities, not only larger numeric bonuses.

## Exploration and resource knowledge

Resource extraction requires exploration and data gathering. Prospecting can reveal composition, concentration, accessibility, uncertainty, hazards, and extraction requirements. Knowledge itself becomes an asset that changes where the economy expands.
