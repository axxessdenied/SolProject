# 08 — Factions and Conflict

## Political actors

The confirmed actor types are Earth nations, private corporations, and later independent colonies. Corporate power should be able to outgrow that of Earth nations as control of off-world resources, transport, and infrastructure shifts influence away from Earth.

## Relationships and power

Potential relationship levers include contracts, licenses, sanctions, taxation, access rights, resource claims, joint ventures, standards, rescue obligations, labor movement, information, and infrastructure dependence.

Power should emerge from capabilities and dependencies—launch capacity, logistics, resources, population, knowledge, and security—not only from a single abstract score.

## Corporate simulation

Other companies may specialize, cooperate, compete for contracts/resources, purchase transport, build assets, and fail or consolidate. Their simulation fidelity should be just sufficient to create credible observable behavior and opportunities.

## Independent colonies

Colonies can develop interests distinct from founders, host nations, or corporate owners. Conditions for independence, player response, and resulting government types remain deferred until the settlement game is designed.

## Piracy and combat

Space is large and interception is difficult. Conflict should respect detection, trajectory, logistics, jurisdiction, and valuable routes. Mid-game piracy is the first proposed threat: theft, coercion, sabotage, hijacking, or attacks near predictable routes/infrastructure.

Broader combat is late-game. It should grow out of an economy worth protecting and begin with detection, escort, hardening, law, and interception before large fleet warfare. Tactical resolution and direct piloting are unresolved.
